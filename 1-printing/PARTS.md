# Printed parts of the foundation robot (2026-10-23)

Generated by `scripts/collect_printed_parts.py` from the release records; do not edit by hand. Two sources make the body: the community print set from AI-FanGe's Microduck build tutorial (`community/microduck-community-print-set.3mf`, MIT, commit 4967821), printed on 2026-10-01 as 89 instances, and our incremental releases V1.6 to V1.31 that replaced or added parts. Releases applied, in order: V1.6, V1.7, V1.8, V1.9, V1.17, V1.20, V1.21, V1.22, V1.24, V1.25, V1.28, V1.31. Later releases (V1.29 saddle, V1.30 USB camera face) are extensions and are not here.

Colour scheme C: white shells, thighs and head cap; yellow lower head shell, jaw and feet; orange rear pack, converter box, backpack and ankles; black links, rings, trim and small parts. Material PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers; print on a Bambu X1C or an Elegoo Centauri Carbon (the per-colour plate files are listed in PLATES.md). Supports: tree supports from the slicer profile where the pose notes say so; brims per part as in PRINT-LESSONS.md.

# 1. Printing

Everything the robot's body is made of, as it stands on 2026-10-23. Print this block first: the servos go into these parts.

## What to print

- `PARTS.md` is the full list, generated by `scripts/collect_printed_parts.py` from the project's release records. It has three sections: our parts by colour (34 STLs under `stl/our-parts/<Colour>/`), the community parts we kept from AI-FanGe's print set (44 STLs under `stl/community/`, 58 instances), and the community and earlier parts we replaced, so you know what not to print. Every entry gives the print pose, why the part exists and the sha256 of the STL.
- `PLATES.md` lists the sliced plates (Bambu Lab X1C `.gcode.3mf` and Elegoo Centauri Carbon `.gcode`, one per colour per release) with sizes and sha256. The files are about 200 MB, so they live in this repository's GitHub Release assets, not in the git tree. If you have one of these two printers, the plates print the parts exactly as ours were printed; otherwise slice the STLs yourself.
- `community/microduck-community-print-set.3mf` is AI-FanGe's original print set (MIT, `community/LICENSE-AI-FanGe`), kept so you can see where the community parts come from. Our `stl/community/community-N.stl` files are the individual objects of that set in their original print pose; `PARTS.md` maps each N to a part name and to the instances the robot uses.
- `PRINT-LESSONS.md` is what went wrong on our printers and how we avoid it: part orientation, supports under overhangs, brims, re-slicing a sliced 3MF, scars on the head cap, why the thigh prints on its side.

## Colours (scheme C)

White: the two torso shells, both thighs, the A7Z head cap and the face panel. Yellow: the lower head shell, the jaw frame and both feet. Orange: the rear pack, the converter box frame and cap, the ankle halves, the clips, the speaker retainer and the battery backpack. Black: the lower-leg links, the ankle sleeve, the neck rings and shims, the jaw support, the eye trim, the sliding ring. Bambu PLA Basic in all four colours, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, four walls.

## How the part set was derived

The robot was printed in twelve releases (V1.6 to V1.31) over five weeks, each replacing a few parts of the previous one. `scripts/collect_printed_parts.py` walks those release manifests in order, applies each release's replacements and retirements, and keeps the parts that remain on the robot (plus the ones a release introduced for the battery route, marked as such). Run it again from a checkout of the project's working tree with `python3 scripts/collect_printed_parts.py --robotics <path to microduck-sim>`; it needs `trimesh` for the few parts whose print pose is a rotation of the assembly model.

Not fitted on the robot although printed: the four wire clips (`clip_*`); the robot's head wires run without them. The two hot-glue sole molds of V1.21 are tools for a route that was dropped (the soles are cut from a bicycle inner tube instead); PARTS.md records them and their STLs are not shipped. The battery backpack frame and cap are for power route B (see `4-power/`), which is not yet on the robot.

Parts sheets (one picture of every part in each release, with sizes) are in `../docs/parts-sheets/`; V1.8 and V1.28 have no sheet because the sheet builder no longer renders those two manifests.

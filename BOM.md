# Bill of materials, CMU MicroDuck foundation (2026-10-23)

Generated by `scripts/build_bom.py` from the project's procurement records (`bom.json`, `catalog-en.json`, `cart-plan.json`, `sourcing-policy.json`, prices as read on the dates recorded there, mostly September and October 2026) and `0-procurement/foundation-additions.json` for the parts bought after those records. Prices are what the records hold, not current quotes. Order numbers and receipts are not published. The sourcing policy of the project is kept: a part on hold or marked club supply was not bought, and this list does not ask you to buy it either; the CMU Robotics Club drawers supplied screws, wire and consumables.

The role text of the club-drawer and tool rows is the September 2026 record and some of it describes the harness of that time (12 V, SMPS2DXL, Raspberry Pi); the part and quantity columns are what the robot uses. Printed parts are in `1-printing/`. Servo count, roles and IDs are in `2-servos-openrb/`.

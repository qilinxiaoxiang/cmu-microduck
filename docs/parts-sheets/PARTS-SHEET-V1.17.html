MicroDuck prints V1.17

9 parts to print, and 10 printed parts they replace. Every other printed part stays.

V1.17 is all of V1.16 plus three head parts for the Radxa Cubie A7Z board and the Radxa 13M 214 camera: a head cap with relieved board rails and a slot at each rail end, a face with four camera bosses, and a cap clip with a notch over the HDMI socket. The black eye trim does not change: reuse the one on the robot. Print only this package; V1.16 was never printed and is replaced.

Print these 9 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

White plate · 6 parts

Left torso shell, screwed to the pack

Replaces the orange left shell. One M2 x 6 goes straight down through the round counterbore in its roof into the rear pack shoulder bar (drill the bar 1.6 mm through the hole first, tape flag 7.0 mm from the tip). No cross tunnel. Four M2 x 4 into the ID12 horn sit in counterbores. Prints on its flat outer face, no brim. 81 × 42 × 34 mm

Right torso shell, screwed to the pack

Replaces the orange right shell. Same pack screw on this side; three M2 x 4 in counterbores into the ID12 idler, the rear hole keeps its M2 x 8 under the clip tab. Prints on its flat outer face, no brim. 81 × 42 × 32 mm

Right thigh

The same thigh in white (colour only). Prints in the 10/1 pose, no brim. 61 × 48 × 28 mm

White head cap for the A7Z

Replaces the orange V1.9 cap. The V1.9 cap with two inside changes for the A7Z: the board rails are cut back 1.6 mm, so the board rests only on a round pad at each of its four holes, and each rail end has a slot, one for the camera connector and ribbon (at the robot's left end), one for the microSD card (at the robot's right end). Speaker collar, amplifier posts, Pi screws and the three M2.6 x 6 closing screws are unchanged. Prints roof down with the 4 mm brim already in the file; the support under the crown is ironed, but sand the matte band lightly and push a needle through each sound hole above the speaker. Clean any support off the rail ends. 123 × 92 × 46 mm

Face panel for the 13M 214 camera

Replaces the printed V1.8 face. Same front; on the back, four round bosses take the Radxa 13M 214 camera with 4 x M2 x 4, and the two holes above and below the eye take the 2 x M2 x 6 that hold the black eye trim from behind. Reuse the trim on the robot. Prints front down, no brim. 88 × 45 × 4 mm

Orange plate · 3 parts

Rear converter box frame

Replaces the printed V1.8 rear pack cover; screws to the pack with the cover's four M2 x 6. Holds two XL4015 boards and the 5991 USB-C trigger, which slide in from the open back; every wire leaves through a side window and stays outside the box (the front plate is solid); the 7 V pair and the Pi lead cross the box top and drop through the small notch at the front edge of the top wall into the pack. Prints standing on its open back rim with tree support inside the box and under the cover flap, no brim. Clean the support off the board boss tips so the boards sit flat. 87 × 64 × 22 mm

Rear converter box cap

New. Rounded cap that closes the back of the box: push it straight on, then drive four M2 x 10 through the round holes in its back: two at the top corners through the 7 V converter's upper holes, two low through the 5991's upper holes. The heads stay hidden inside the cap. No ears, no tongues, no hook, nothing outside the box outline. Prints on its flat back face with tree support under the rounded edges. 79 × 64 × 15 mm

Cap cable clip with HDMI notch

Replaces the orange V1.9 cap clip. Same clip with a notch in its underside so the arm clears the A7Z micro HDMI socket; same M2 x 8. Prints flat, no brim; on the X1C file a little support fills the notch, pick it out. 18 × 13 × 8 mm

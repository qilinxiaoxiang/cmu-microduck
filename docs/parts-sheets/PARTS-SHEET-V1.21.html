MicroDuck prints V1.21

2 parts to print, and they replace no printed part. Every other printed part stays.

V1.21 is one plate with two tools: the hot-glue molds for the left and right foot soles. They are not robot parts, so any PLA spool works. Wipe a thin release film into the cavity, fill it with hot glue, press the sanded foot between the four tabs onto the three pins, let it cool fully, then lift straight up.

Print these 2 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

Orange plate · 2 parts

Left foot sole mold

For the left foot (community-44). Raised L at the heel end; the top of the letter points to the toe. The cavity leaves a 1.0 mm glue coat over the flat and up the rounded edge to 3 mm. Prints cavity up, 0.12 mm layers with ironing, no support, no brim. 75 × 55 × 16 mm

Right foot sole mold

For the right foot (community-46), mirror of the left mold, with a raised R at the heel end, top of the letter to the toe. Same coat and print settings. 75 × 55 × 16 mm

MicroDuck prints V1.22

8 parts to print, and 6 printed parts they replace. Every other printed part stays.

V1.22 is three plates and reworks both ankles. Orange: an ankle base and a removable ankle side for each leg; the base sits on the servo horn and takes the lower link with its servo from above, the side slides on along the shaft and two M2 x 8 hold it. Black: a new sleeve for each leg, 10.4 mm bore, that turns freely on the link instead of being pressed. Yellow: three pairs of new feet that slide onto the base by hand and are held by the toe M2 x 6. One pair goes on the robot; the other two are spares for sole experiments, since a foot comes off with its one toe screw. Nothing in the ankle is spread or pressed any more.

Print these 8 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

Yellow plate · 2 parts

Left foot, slip fit

Replaces the left foot. The socket for the ankle block is 0.2 mm wider on every side and the two snap hooks are gone, so the foot slides straight up onto the base and side by hand. Then drive the M2 x 6 from above through the toe of the base into the foot. If it is still tight, sand the socket walls of the foot; never press it on. Prints on its sole, as before. 54 × 41 × 18 mm

Right foot, slip fit

Replaces the right foot. Same slip fit and the same M2 x 6 at the toe. Prints on its sole, as before. 54 × 41 × 18 mm

Orange plate · 4 parts

Left ankle base

With the left ankle side, replaces the left ankle bracket. Screws to the servo horn with the four M2 x 4 as before and carries the foot block. With the side off, the lower link with its servo and sleeve sits straight down into it; nothing is spread. 43 × 36 × 25 mm

Right ankle base

With the right ankle side, replaces the right ankle bracket. Same as the left base, mirrored. 43 × 36 × 25 mm

Left ankle side

The removable side of the left ankle, on the inner face (towards the other leg). Slide it along the shaft, away from the centre line, onto the sleeve until its two tabs meet the two posts of the base, then drive two M2 x 8 from above, hand snug (they cut their own thread). The foot, fitted afterwards, holds it in; take the foot off before opening it. 30 × 25 × 9 mm

Right ankle side

The removable side of the right ankle. Same as the left side, mirrored. 30 × 25 × 9 mm

Black plate · 2 parts

Left ankle sleeve

Replaces the left ankle sleeve. Bore 10.4 mm: it slides onto the 10 mm boss of the lower link by hand and turns freely. Put it on the link before the link goes into the base. 15 × 15 × 3 mm

Right ankle sleeve

Replaces the right ankle sleeve. Same as the left sleeve. 15 × 15 × 3 mm

Replace these

Take each ankle apart once, with the ankle-and-foot module off the knee: toe screw out and the foot rocked off (hold the orange bracket, not the leg or the servo; the foot may crack), then the four M2 x 4 output screws, the two M2 x 10 case screws and the M2.6 x 8 rear centre with its washer. Pull the link off the bracket evenly and lift the servo out. Keep every screw and the washer; only the printed parts below are replaced.

MicroDuck prints V1.24

4 parts to print, and 4 printed parts they replace. Every other printed part stays.

V1.24 is one orange plate: each ankle is the stock bracket cut in half in the middle. The outer half screws to the servo horn with the four M2 x 4; the inner half slides along the shaft onto the black sleeve. The foot holds both: its socket wraps the two halves, and the toe M2 x 6 sits on the line where they meet and holds both down. No posts and no M2 x 8. The V1.22 black sleeves and yellow slip-fit feet stay.

Print these 4 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

Orange plate · 4 parts

Left ankle outer half

The outer half of the left ankle: the plate on the servo horn and half of the foot block. Screws to the horn with the four M2 x 4 as before; the lower link with its servo and sleeve sits straight down into it. After printing: cut the thin brim strip out of the small notch on the end face that stood on the bed; on an Elegoo print also pull any support tips out of the four screw counterbores so the screw heads sit flat. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Left ankle inner half

The inner half of the left ankle, on the face towards the other leg: the plate with the sleeve pocket and the other half of the foot block. Slide it along the shaft, away from the centre line, onto the sleeve until it meets the outer half; then slide the foot on and drive the toe M2 x 6. It prints on its heel corner with the round sleeve pocket free of support (the pocket has a pointed top for that); break the support off under the foot block. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Right ankle outer half

The outer half of the right ankle. Same as the left, mirrored. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Right ankle inner half

The inner half of the right ankle. Same as the left, mirrored. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Replace these

On each leg, with the ankle-and-foot module off the knee: toe screw out and the foot slid down, the two M2 x 8 out of the side (they are not used again) and the side slid off towards the centre line, then the four M2 x 4 output screws. Lift the link with its servo and sleeve out of the base. Keep the sleeve, the feet and the other screws.

MicroDuck prints V1.25

4 parts to print, and 2 printed parts they replace. Every other printed part stays.

V1.25 is the V1.24 orange plate again with a round sleeve bore in both inner halves. Support stands inside the bore while it prints; it sits on the heel side of the bore, away from the side that carries the robot. The outer halves are the same as in V1.24; a good printed V1.24 outer half can be used instead of a new one. Every half has a wider brim (8 mm) so the tall parts stay on the bed.

Print these 4 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

Orange plate · 4 parts

Left ankle outer half

The outer half of the left ankle: the plate on the servo horn and half of the foot block. Screws to the horn with the four M2 x 4 as before; the lower link with its servo and sleeve sits straight down into it. After printing: cut the thin brim strip out of the small notch on the end face that stood on the bed; on an Elegoo print also pull any support tips out of the four screw counterbores so the screw heads sit flat. Peel the 8 mm brim off all round. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Left ankle inner half

The inner half of the left ankle, on the face towards the other leg: the plate with the round sleeve bore and the other half of the foot block. Slide it along the shaft, away from the centre line, onto the sleeve until it meets the outer half; then slide the foot on and drive the toe M2 x 6. It prints standing on its toe end with support inside the bore: break the support out, then scrape the bore and the flat lip face inside it smooth until the black sleeve turns freely in it. Peel the 8 mm brim off and cut the thin brim strip out of the small notch on the end face that stood on the bed; on an Elegoo print also pick the support tips out of the toe screw countersink. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Right ankle outer half

The outer half of the right ankle. Same as the left, mirrored. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Right ankle inner half

The inner half of the right ankle. Same as the left, mirrored. 36 × 25 × 20 mm

Replace these

If a V1.24 inner half is already fitted: foot off (toe screw out, foot slid down), then slide the inner half off along the shaft towards the centre line. Keep the sleeve, the foot and the screw.
